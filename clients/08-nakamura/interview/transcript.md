## Transcript Notes

This transcript features a financially sophisticated couple — George is a CPA — who understand tax optimization and articulate their plan with unusual precision. The system must handle technical financial language, the distinction between traditional IRA and Roth IRA treatment, and the concept of routing charitable gifts through the tax-advantaged vehicle. The couple also has a nuanced situation with Ryan's partner Sam — they want to acknowledge him but are unsure how to do it within the legal framework, and ultimately decide on a specific bequest with a personal letter outside the document.

George leads on the financial details and tax strategy. Patricia leads on family relationships and emotional considerations. They finish each other's thoughts frequently. George occasionally uses accounting terminology that the attorney and Patricia gently translate.
