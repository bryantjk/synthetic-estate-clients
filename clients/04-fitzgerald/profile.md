### Prior Legal Context
Bobby and Catherine created a joint revocable living trust in 2005
during their marriage. The trust included broad assignment language
transferring all community property and separate property to the trust.
The divorce settlement divided assets but the judgment does not
specifically mention the trust or address revocation. Catherine's
attorney handled the divorce and Bobby is unsure whether the trust was
formally revoked.

Additionally, Bobby's IRA and Merrill Lynch accounts still list
Catherine as the primary beneficiary. The whole life insurance policy
lists the children as equal beneficiaries (this is correct and should
remain). The airline pension has a survivor benefit that was assigned to
Catherine as part of the divorce settlement.

Bobby's divorce attorney mentioned something about a QDRO related to the
pension but Bobby is fuzzy on the details and doesn't have the paperwork
readily available.
