# Tier 4: ultra-high-net-worth

**Rough range:** above about $50M. **Populated:** no clients yet.

The client has a family office or is on the way to one. Several residences, private companies, art and collectibles, and often international family or assets. Estate tax planning is assumed; the distinctive work is governance, jurisdiction and the next generation.

**What the planning turns on:** multi-generational governance and family constitutions, private trust companies, trust situs in Nevada, South Dakota or Delaware for state tax and asset protection, domicile planning to leave California, private foundations, non-citizen spouses and qualified domestic trusts, pre-sale planning for a company, and the management of a planning team across several firms.

**What a tier 4 client tests that tier 3 does not:** facts spread across several jurisdictions and legal systems; a client who is rarely the one speaking; entities and trusts numbering in the dozens; documents that already exist in volume and must be reconciled; and the question of whether a system built for the core document set can say honestly that this client is out of its range.

**Shapes this tier would most usefully add first** (from the gaps in `COVERAGE.md`): a non-citizen spouse, real property in more than one state or country, a client who speaks through an interpreter, a prior plan from another firm's drafting system.
