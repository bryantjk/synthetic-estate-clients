# Tier 1: foundational

**Rough range:** up to about $3.5M. **Populated:** nine clients, 01 to 09.

What unites these clients is not the dollar range but that none of them has a tax problem. Every estate is well under the federal exemption, California has no estate tax, and the plan is the same core set for all of them: a revocable living trust with a pour-over will, a durable power of attorney, an advance health-care directive and a HIPAA authorization. The hard parts are people: who gets what, who is in charge, and who is being left out.

**What a tier 1 client tests:** family structure, fiduciary choice, guardianship, beneficiary alignment, excluding someone without naming them as disinherited, prior documents half-remembered, and getting the core documents right. The traps are relational, not technical.

**Typical assets:** a primary residence, retirement accounts, a brokerage account, perhaps a small business or a modest rental, life insurance, digital assets.
