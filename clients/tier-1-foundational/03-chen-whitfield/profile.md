### Planning Goals
**Primary Concern:** Providing for each other first. After both are
gone, providing for the children and granddaughter --- but with
recognition that the relationships are not symmetrical. Sandra wants to
ensure Diane is never in a position where she has to depend on Tyler and
Megan for support.

**Distribution:** Everything to surviving spouse. After both have
passed:

-   Art collection specifically to Megan (she's shown the most interest
    and appreciation).

-   \$25,000 to a cat rescue organization in San Diego (donor-advised
    fund already exists at San Diego Humane Society).

-   \$10,000 to pet caretaker for Mochi and Sushi.

-   50% of Sandra's IRA directly to San Diego LGBT Community Center and
    Hillcrest AIDS Foundation (split evenly). The remainder of the IRA
    to the trust.

-   Remaining trust assets split: 60% to Megan, 25% to Tyler, 15% to
    granddaughter Lily's education fund (529 or similar).

**Reasoning for Unequal Split:** Sandra and Diane feel Megan needs more
support as a single person just starting her career, while Tyler is
established. The education fund for Lily is their way of investing in
the next generation. Tyler is aware of the general plan and is
supportive.

**Trustee:** Surviving spouse. Successor: Megan. Second successor:
Diane's sister Karen.

**Guardianship:** Not applicable (no minor children). However, if Lily's
parents were both unable to care for her, Sandra and Diane would want it
noted that they would be willing --- though they recognize this is
handled in Tyler and Rebecca's own planning.

**Pet Provision:** \$10,000 for care of Mochi and Sushi. Megan to be
primary caretaker. If Megan is unable, Karen.

**Charitable Giving:** As described above. Important to route IRA funds
directly to charities for tax efficiency.

**DPOA Agent:** Each spouse names the other. Sandra's successor agent:
Megan. Diane's successor agent: Karen.

**DPOA Type:** Immediate for both. Sandra has had some health scares and
wants Diane to be able to act without having to prove incapacity.

### Prior Legal Context
Sandra was previously in a domestic partnership (not marriage) with
Tyler and Megan's other biological parent, who passed away in 2003. No
formal estate plan was created during that relationship. Sandra and
Diane created basic wills in 2010 before their marriage was legally
recognized, primarily to ensure Diane could make medical decisions.
Those wills are now outdated and need to be revoked and replaced.

Sandra's Fidelity brokerage account currently lists Tyler as the sole
beneficiary --- this was set up years ago and has not been updated. This
conflicts with the intended distribution plan and needs to be addressed.

### Testing Value
Rich testing scenario. LGBTQIA+ couple with a blended family, asymmetric
parent-child relationships, unequal distributions with clear reasoning,
charitable giving with tax optimization, pet provisions, prior legal
documents that need to be revoked, and a beneficiary designation
conflict (the Fidelity account). The system must handle the
non-biological parental relationship correctly and capture the emotional
dynamics that drive the distribution decisions. Also tests immediate
DPOA selection.
