### Planning Goals
**Primary Concern:** Having something in place after seeing what
happened when his father passed without a plan. Doesn't want his mother
and sister to go through that again.

**Distribution:** Everything to his mother Gloria. If Gloria has
predeceased, everything to Isabella. If both have predeceased, to Ash.

**Ash's Role:** Tomás wants Ash involved in his planning but recognizes
they aren't married. He wants Ash to be his agent for both financial and
healthcare decisions. He trusts them completely and wants to ensure Ash
has legal authority to act if something happens, since as an unmarried
partner they would have no default legal standing.

**Digital Assets:** Tomás has cryptocurrency and wants to make sure
someone can access it. He also has a digital art portfolio and a side
project (an app in development) that he considers intellectually and
financially valuable. He wants to ensure these are properly addressed.

**Trustee:** Gloria. Successor: Isabella. Second successor: Ash.

**DPOA Agent:** Ash. Successor: Gloria.

**DPOA Type:** Immediate. Tomás wants Ash to be able to act without
barriers.

**Body Disposition:** No strong preference. "Whatever is easiest and
cheapest for my family."

### Prior Legal Context
No prior estate planning documents. No prior marriages. The 401(k)
beneficiary designation currently lists his mother, which aligns with
his wishes. The Coinbase account has no beneficiary designation
mechanism --- access would require login credentials.

### Testing Value
Tests the youngest and simplest end of the client spectrum, but with
meaningful nuances. The unmarried partner with nonbinary identity tests
the system's pronoun and relationship handling. Digital assets (crypto,
app IP) are an increasingly common concern. The emotional motivation
(father's death without a plan) is context the system should capture as
it informs the urgency and priorities. Also tests the scenario where the
DPOA agent is different from the primary beneficiary --- Ash is the
agent but Gloria is the primary beneficiary.
