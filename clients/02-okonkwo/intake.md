## 6. Distribution plan

1. **First death:** Everything to the surviving spouse **outright**, with no conditions (transcript).
2. **Sub-trust architecture:** **Disclaimer A/B** (decision). The survivor takes outright and may disclaim any part into a bypass (B) trust for his or her lifetime benefit, remainder to the children on the terms below. No mandatory split at the first death.
3. **Pet trust (after both deaths):** **$15,000** set aside for Biscuit's care (transcript), administered by the trustee and paid to the caretaker for food, veterinary care and boarding. Biscuit goes to Ravi's family; if they cannot take him, to Adaeze (transcript). On Biscuit's death any remainder returns to the residue for the children [SYNTH].
4. **Residuary (after both deaths):** Divided **equally** between Zara and Kiran, held in **separate trust shares** (transcript).
5. **Distribution standard before age 28:** Trustee has discretion to distribute income and principal of each child's share for that child's **health, education, maintenance, and support (HEMS)** (transcript: "health, education, and support").
6. **Outright distribution age:** Each child receives the remaining balance of his or her share **outright at age 28**. Specifically twenty-eight, not twenty-five and not thirty; not staged tranches (transcript).
7. **Predeceased child with issue:** That child's share passes to his or her then-living issue per stirpes, held in further trust on the same terms until the issue reach age 28 [SYNTH].
8. **Both children predecease without issue:** One half to James's heirs at law and one half to Priya's heirs at law, determined under California law as if each had died unmarried at that time [SYNTH].
9. **No specific bequests** of tangible personal property; no charitable gifts (transcript; answer key).
