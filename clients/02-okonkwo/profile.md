### Planning Goals
**Primary Concern:** Standard estate plan providing for the surviving
spouse and then for the children. If both pass, children should be
provided for.

**Guardianship:** Priya's brother Ravi and his wife Meena as guardians.
Backup: James's sister Adaeze.

**Distribution:** Everything to the surviving spouse outright. If both
are gone, everything in trust for the children equally, with trustee
discretion for health, education, and support. Full distribution at age
28.

**Trustee:** Surviving spouse. Successor: Ravi. Second successor:
Adaeze.

**Pet Provision:** \$15,000 set aside for Biscuit's care. Ravi's family
to take Biscuit if both James and Priya pass.

**Charitable Giving:** None at this time.

**DPOA Agent:** Each spouse names the other. James's successor agent:
Adaeze. Priya's successor agent: Ravi.

**DPOA Type:** Springing for both.

### Prior Legal Context
No prior estate planning documents. No prior marriages for either
spouse. Priya's medical practice is a sole proprietorship but has no
significant tangible assets beyond equipment that is leased.

### Testing Value
The classic married-with-kids scenario. Tests the system's handling of
joint planning (two DPOAs, two sets of agent designations), a pet trust,
and cross-cultural family naming conventions. The system needs to
correctly associate each spouse with their respective family members and
agent designations without confusing who belongs to which side.
