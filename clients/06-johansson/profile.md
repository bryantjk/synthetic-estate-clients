### Planning Goals
**Primary Concern:** Providing for both children, but structuring
Peter's inheritance to protect him from himself during any future
relapse. She loves Peter deeply and does not want to punish him --- she
wants to support his recovery while safeguarding the assets.

**Distribution:**

-   Erik's woodworking tools and projects to Peter.

-   Scandinavian furniture collection to Anna.

-   \$5,000 for Whiskers's care to Anna (informal, not a separate pet
    trust given the cat's age).

-   \$20,000 each to grandchildren Emma and Jack for education.

-   \$25,000 to her church (First Lutheran Church of Coronado).

-   Coronado house to Anna (Anna and Miguel have expressed interest in
    purchasing it eventually, but Linda wants them to have it).

-   Remaining assets split 50/50 between Anna and Peter.

**Peter's Share:** Linda wants Peter's 50% held in a discretionary trust
rather than distributed outright. The trustee would have authority to
make distributions for Peter's health, education, maintenance, and
support, but with the discretion to limit or withhold distributions if
Peter has relapsed. She does not want specific "sobriety testing"
language --- she finds that degrading. She trusts the trustee to use
good judgment. If Peter passes before the trust is fully distributed,
remaining assets go to Anna's children equally.

**Trustee:** Anna as primary trustee for Peter's trust and as successor
trustee for the overall trust. Linda has thought about whether it's a
conflict for Anna to be trustee of Peter's trust while also being a
beneficiary of the overall estate, but she trusts Anna completely.

**DPOA Agent:** Anna. Successor: Anna's husband Miguel.

**DPOA Type:** Springing.

**Body Disposition:** Buried next to Erik at Fort Rosecrans National
Cemetery. Already has a plot reserved.

### Prior Legal Context
Linda and Erik had a joint revocable living trust created in 2015. After
Erik's death, the trust was administered and Erik's share was
distributed to Linda per the trust terms. The trust still exists and is
technically still operative in Linda's name alone, but the distribution
provisions reflect the old plan (everything to the surviving spouse,
then equally to children outright). Linda wants the trust restated, not
revoked and replaced, to maintain continuity and the existing property
assignments.

The Coronado house was transferred out of joint title into Linda's name
after Erik's passing but was not re-titled into the trust. This needs to
be addressed. The IRA beneficiary designation currently lists Peter and
Anna equally --- this may or may not align with the new plan depending
on how the discretionary trust for Peter is structured.

### Testing Value
Tests a complex emotional scenario: a parent wanting to protect a child
with substance use issues without being punitive. The discretionary
trust for Peter is a nuanced provision. The system must capture Linda's
strong feelings about not including sobriety testing language while
still creating a structure that protects Peter. Also tests trust
restatement vs. revocation, a property that fell out of the trust, IRA
beneficiary designation alignment, and multi-generational distributions
with specific bequests.
