# Tier 3: taxable estate

**Rough range:** the federal exemption up to about $50M. **Populated:** no clients yet.

Federal estate tax is a real cost, typically at the 40 percent marginal rate on the excess over the exemption, and planning is organised around reducing it. The client usually has a business with partners, a pending liquidity event, or inherited wealth. The attorney works alongside a CPA and a financial advisor, and the interview is often with the whole team rather than the client alone.

**What the planning turns on:** lifetime gifting against the exemption, spousal lifetime access trusts, grantor retained annuity trusts, sales to intentionally defective grantor trusts, dynasty trusts with generation-skipping transfer tax allocation, valuation discounts through family entities, charitable lead and remainder trusts, donor-advised funds or a private foundation, buy-sell agreements, and coordination of the whole with the income tax picture.

**What a tier 3 client tests that tier 2 does not:** a multi-session engagement rather than a single interview; advisors speaking for the client and using technical vocabulary that may or may not be right; a plan design with several irrevocable trusts and entities that must be read as a system; existing trusts that the new plan must work around rather than replace; the difference between what the client wants and what the tax plan requires.

**Shapes this tier would most usefully add first** (from the gaps in `COVERAGE.md`): an estate above the federal exemption where tax planning drives the structure, a business with partners, a family farm or ranch whose value is in keeping it together.
