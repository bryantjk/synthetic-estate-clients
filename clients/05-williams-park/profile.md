### Planning Goals
**Primary Concern:** Providing for each other. After both have passed,
giving back to the communities that supported them.

**Distribution:** Everything to the surviving spouse. After both have
passed:

-   \$30,000 pet trust for Bowie and Prince (and any future pets).

-   The restaurant and its assets to Andre's sous chef, Miguel Reyes,
    who has worked with Andre for 12 years and is like a younger brother
    to him. Andre wants the business to continue.

-   \$50,000 to Lisa Nakamura.

-   \$25,000 to Marcus's mother Helen, if living, for care needs.

-   60% of remaining assets to charitable organizations: San Diego LGBT
    Community Center (25%), Trevor Project (25%), a local scholarship
    fund for LGBTQIA+ youth (10%).

-   40% of remaining assets to Denise (Marcus's sister).

**Trustee:** Surviving spouse. Successor: Lisa Nakamura. Second
successor: Denise.

**Restaurant Succession:** Andre wants this handled cleanly. Miguel
should have the option to continue operating with no financial burden.
If Miguel declines, the restaurant should be sold and proceeds go to the
charitable pool.

**Pet Provision:** \$30,000 for care of current and future dogs. Lisa
Nakamura to be primary caretaker. Backup: Denise.

**DPOA Agent:** Each spouse names the other. Marcus's successor agent:
Denise. Andre's successor agent: Lisa Nakamura.

**DPOA Type:** Immediate for both. Andre is especially concerned about
potential difficulty if his biological family were to try to intervene.

### Prior Legal Context
Marcus and Andre had a domestic partnership before marriage. They
created basic wills in 2013 that were drafted by a friend who was a law
student at the time. The wills are poorly drafted and don't reflect
current wishes. Neither has a trust. Andre's restaurant has no formal
succession plan or operating agreement.

Andre is particularly concerned about the legal vulnerability of having
no proper documents. His biological family, though estranged, has not
formally waived any inheritance rights, and he wants to ensure they have
no claim on his estate.

### Testing Value
Tests chosen family dynamics (Lisa's role), estrangement with legal
implications, a small business succession concern, significant
charitable giving, and the interplay between a domestic partnership and
subsequent marriage. The biological family concern is an important
capture point --- the system should flag that Andre may want explicit
language addressing the exclusion of his biological relatives, even
though he didn't use the word "disinherit." Also tests immediate DPOA
and the concept of future pets in the pet trust.
