## Transcript Notes

This transcript features a married gay couple where one partner (Andre) is estranged from his biological family and has built a chosen family network instead. The system must handle the concept of chosen family — specifically Lisa Nakamura, who fills the role that a sibling or parent would in traditional planning but has no legal relationship. Andre also owns a small restaurant with an informal succession concern. Both partners choose immediate DPOA, with Andre's choice driven by specific fear that his estranged biological family could intervene.

Marcus is calm and measured. Andre is more animated and emotional, especially about his family estrangement and the restaurant. They occasionally disagree on details and correct each other.
