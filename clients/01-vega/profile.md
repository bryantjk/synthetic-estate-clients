### Planning Goals
**Primary Concern:** If something happens to her, making sure the
children are cared for and financially provided for. This is her
overwhelming priority. She is a single mother with no co-parent
involvement --- the children's father is not in the picture and she
wants him to have no role.

**Guardianship:** Wants her mother Rosa to be guardian of the children.
If Rosa is unable, her brother Carlos and his wife Ana should serve. She
is very close to her sister-in-law Ana and trusts her judgment with the
kids.

**Distribution:** Everything to the children equally. If both children
are minors, assets held in trust with distributions for health,
education, and support. Remaining assets distributed outright at age 25.

**Trustee:** Brother Carlos as successor trustee. If Carlos is unable,
her close friend Jennifer Park.

**DPOA Agent:** Mother Rosa. Successor: Carlos.

**DPOA Type:** Springing.

### Prior Legal Context
No prior estate planning documents. No prior marriages or divorces.
Children's father is not on the birth certificates. No custody
agreements.

### Testing Value
Straightforward single-parent scenario. Tests the system's ability to
handle guardianship designations, minor beneficiaries with age-based
conditions, and the distinction between guardian and trustee roles. The
absent father adds a nuance --- the system should capture the client's
explicit wish to exclude someone without that person being a formal
"disinherited" party.
